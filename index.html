About the app

Built for students and everyday budgets

SpendWise is built around a simple idea: you can only manage money you can see. Everything you record stays on your phone. There is no sign-up, no bank linking, and no advertising.

100%

Offline. Your data stays on your device.

Free

No subscription and no hidden paywall.

Kotlin

Built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3.

Income and expenses at a glance

Every transaction shows its type with a colored bar and tag, so you never misread a row. Add, edit, search and filter from one list.

See where the money goes

A category breakdown shows which areas take the biggest share of your spending, from food and travel to bills and education.

Track trends over months

Compare this month with previous ones to see whether your spending is rising, falling, or staying steady.

Reminders that clean up after themselves

Set a due date and time for a bill or goal. Once the time passes, the reminder removes itself, so your list only shows what matters.
